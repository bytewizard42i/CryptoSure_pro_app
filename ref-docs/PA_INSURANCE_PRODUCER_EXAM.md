# Pennsylvania Insurance Producer Examination

## Authoritative sources

- **Regulator:** Pennsylvania Insurance Department
- **Official licensing process:** [Initial Insurance Producer Licensing Process](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/insurance/licensing/licensees/initial-insurance-producer-licensing-process)
- **Exam administrator:** PSI Services
- **Official scheduling page:** [Pennsylvania Insurance Examinations](https://test-takers.psiexams.com/pain)
- **Candidate Information Bulletin:** [Official PSI examination bulletin](https://test-takers.psiexams.com/api/content/bulletin/7329)
- **Approved education lookup:** [Sircon Pennsylvania](https://www.sircon.com/pennsylvania)
- **Verified:** July 21, 2026

## Plain-English summary

The likely starting license for CryptoSure is Property and Casualty, subject to written
confirmation from the Pennsylvania Insurance Department and retained counsel. Property
insurance concerns things that can be lost or damaged. Casualty insurance largely
concerns legal responsibility when someone causes damage, injury, or financial loss.

The current official candidate bulletin says an initial Pennsylvania insurance producer
applicant normally completes 24 hours of approved pre-license education before taking
the examination. The combined Property and Casualty producer examination is identified
as Series 16-06. The bulletin currently lists 150 questions, 170 minutes, a 70 percent
passing score, and a $53 examination fee. Fees and procedures must be confirmed on the
live scheduling page before payment.

## Basic eligibility listed in the candidate bulletin

A resident producer applicant generally must:

- be at least 18 years old;
- maintain a principal residence or place of business in Pennsylvania;
- demonstrate the required professional competence, general fitness, and integrity;
- be able to read and write English, with permitted accessibility aids;
- complete applicable pre-license education;
- pass the required examination; and
- complete the application and fingerprint process.

The listed requirements do not include a college degree or previous insurance
employment. Certain professional designations, existing licenses, or business-entity
applications may qualify for exemptions, but CryptoSure should not assume an exemption.

## Important sequence

1. Confirm the correct line of authority with the Pennsylvania Insurance Department.
2. Complete an approved 24-hour pre-license course.
3. Schedule and pass the examination through PSI Services.
4. Apply for the individual producer license through Sircon or the National Insurance
   Producer Registry.
5. Complete fingerprinting only after passing applicable examinations and submitting the
   license application, as directed by the Department.
6. Complete business-entity licensing and carrier appointment steps separately.

## Acronym decoder

- **PSI:** The examination company administering Pennsylvania's insurance tests. The
  letters are used as the company's name and are not normally expanded by the company.
- **Property and Casualty (P&C):** Insurance for property and for many forms of legal
  liability.
- **National Insurance Producer Registry (NIPR):** A national online system used for
  insurance license applications and renewals.
- **Candidate Information Bulletin (CIB):** The official examination handbook containing
  procedures, fees, and test-content outlines.
