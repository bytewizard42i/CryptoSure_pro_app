# Pennsylvania Keystone Smart Launch

## Authoritative source

- **Publisher:** Pennsylvania Insurance Department
- **Official page:** [Request a Preliminary Review of a New Insurance Product - Keystone Smart Launch Program](https://www.pa.gov/services/insurance/request-a-preliminary-review-of-a-new-insurance-product-keystone-smart-launch-program)
- **Verified:** July 21, 2026
- **Contact:** `RA-IN-INNOVATEINTAKE@pa.gov`

## Why this is a source of truth for CryptoSure

Keystone Smart Launch is Pennsylvania's official entry point for innovators seeking
preliminary feedback on a new insurance product or service. The Department states that
the program is available to regulated entities, nonregulated entities, innovators,
startups, and disruptors. Its submission process can offer confidentiality.

CryptoSure should use this program to ask how Pennsylvania classifies the proposed
technology, producer, program-administration, underwriting, claims, wallet-monitoring,
education, and forensic-recovery activities before live quoting, binding, premium
collection, or policy issuance.

## Important boundary

Keystone Smart Launch provides preliminary regulatory feedback. It is not itself an
insurance license, carrier appointment, policy approval, rate approval, certificate of
authority, or permission to sell coverage. CryptoSure still needs the licenses,
contracts, filings, and approvals that the Department and retained counsel identify.
