## Best non-insurance wedge

The fastest responsible entry may be a paid or sponsored **CryptoSure Protection Membership** that includes education, wallet-health checks, incident-preparation templates, emergency triage, and negotiated forensic access. It must be drafted so customers understand that it is not an insurance policy and does not guarantee reimbursement or asset recovery.

This wedge can build:

- a consented security-controls dataset;
- customer-acquisition evidence;
- incident frequency and response-time data;
- trusted partner workflows;
- brand familiarity;
- a population that may later qualify for carrier-backed coverage.

Counsel should classify the membership and all advertising before launch.

## Underwriter-facing differentiators

1. **Versioned scope:** every policy or simulation binds to the exact coverage language the user saw.
2. **Privacy-aware evidence:** prove only what the carrier needs while retaining lawful claims access.
3. **Continuous controls:** observe whether required security posture remains in place.
4. **Fast incident triage:** begin evidence preservation and tracing immediately.
5. **Recovery accounting:** treat recovered assets as claims salvage or subrogation under explicit policy rules.
6. **Accumulation monitoring:** identify shared protocols, bridges, custodians, wallet software, and infrastructure dependencies.
7. **Education with acceptance:** record completion and acknowledgement without claiming education eliminates risk.

## Prioritization test

An idea moves forward only if it can answer yes to all six questions:

1. Is the covered or delivered benefit unambiguous?
2. Can eligibility and loss be evidenced without collecting dangerous amounts of data?
3. Can correlated exposure be measured and capped?
4. Can a carrier, regulator, customer, and claims adjuster understand it the same way?
5. Can the economics survive claims, recovery cost, distribution, compliance, and support?
6. Can the team operate it safely during a stressful real incident?
