# Beginner Guide to the Pennsylvania Insurance Producer Exam

## Which exam is most relevant?

CryptoSure will likely need at least one individual with Pennsylvania Property and
Casualty producer authority, but the Pennsylvania Insurance Department and retained
counsel should confirm the exact line of authority before anyone pays for training.

**Property and Casualty (P&C)** means insurance covering physical or digital property
and many forms of legal responsibility for losses. Pennsylvania's combined examination
is currently identified as **Series 16-06**.

## Are there prerequisites?

For a typical initial resident producer applicant:

- You must be at least 18 years old.
- Your main residence or place of business must be in Pennsylvania.
- You must meet the Department's fitness, competence, and character requirements.
- You must be able to read and write English, with accessibility aids when applicable.
- You normally complete 24 hours of approved pre-license education.
- You must pass the applicable examination.
- You must apply for the license and complete the required fingerprint process.

You do not need a college degree, an accounting degree, or previous insurance work. An
insurance or accounting background can make the material easier, but it does not replace
the required course, examination, application, or background review.

If an applicant has a potentially disqualifying criminal history, Pennsylvania offers an
optional confidential preliminary eligibility review before the applicant spends money
on education and testing.

## Where to begin

1. Read the repository's
   [official examination source record](../ref-docs/PA_INSURANCE_PRODUCER_EXAM.md).
2. Confirm the proposed Property and Casualty line with the Pennsylvania Insurance
   Department at `ra-in-producer@pa.gov` or 717-787-3840.
3. Visit [Sircon Pennsylvania](https://www.sircon.com/pennsylvania), choose the approved
   course lookup, select Pennsylvania, and look for approved pre-license education.
4. Choose a course that includes the Pennsylvania law section and multiple timed
   practice examinations.
5. Download the current PSI Services Candidate Information Bulletin and use the Series
   16-06 content outline as the study checklist.
6. Schedule only after practice scores are consistently above the minimum passing score.

## A beginner-friendly four-week study plan

### Week 1: learn the language

- policy, premium, deductible, limit, exclusion, endorsement, and claim;
- insurer, insured, producer, broker, underwriter, and adjuster;
- risk, peril, hazard, loss, indemnity, insurable interest, and subrogation;
- how an application becomes a policy.

Do not merely memorize definitions. Write one plain-English example for every term.

### Week 2: property insurance

- covered property and covered causes of loss;
- replacement cost versus actual cash value;
- homeowners, dwelling, renters, commercial property, inland marine, and crime;
- deductibles, limits, conditions, exclusions, and endorsements.

**Actual cash value** usually accounts for depreciation. **Replacement cost** generally
focuses on the cost to replace damaged property under the policy rules.

### Week 3: casualty and liability insurance

- negligence and legal liability;
- personal and commercial automobile coverage;
- commercial general liability;
- workers' compensation;
- professional and cyber liability concepts;
- bonds, umbrella coverage, and excess coverage.

Casualty questions often ask who was legally responsible, what triggered coverage, and
which policy section responds.

### Week 4: Pennsylvania law and timed practice

- licensing and appointments;
- unfair insurance practices;
- fiduciary handling of premium money;
- advertising and misrepresentation rules;
- privacy, fraud, claims, cancellation, and renewal rules;
- full timed practice examinations followed by review of every wrong answer.

Spend extra time on Pennsylvania-specific law. The official outline assigns 20 percent
of the combined examination to insurance regulation.

## Current examination snapshot

The current official bulletin lists the combined Property and Casualty Series 16-06 as:

- 150 questions;
- 170 minutes;
- 70 percent minimum passing score; and
- $53 examination fee.

These details can change. Confirm them on the
[official PSI Services scheduling page](https://test-takers.psiexams.com/pain) before
registering.

## How to know when you are ready

- Complete the required approved course.
- Score at least 80 percent on several fresh practice examinations.
- Explain every missed question in plain English.
- Recognize why the wrong answers are wrong.
- Complete a 150-question practice session within the time limit.
- Review the Pennsylvania law section again during the final two days.

## What happens after passing?

1. Apply through Sircon or the **National Insurance Producer Registry (NIPR)**.
2. Complete fingerprinting through IdentoGO only after the examination and application,
   following Pennsylvania's sequence.
3. Wait for the Department's review and license issuance.
4. Complete the business-entity producer application separately.
5. Obtain the insurer appointment or other authority required for the actual product.
6. After licensing, complete 24 hours of **continuing education (CE)** during each
   two-year license cycle.

Passing the test makes someone eligible to apply. It does not itself authorize the
person to sell insurance before the Department issues the license.
